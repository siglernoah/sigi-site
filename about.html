04About

What Signet Works is all about

Signet Works is one person: me. Here’s who I am, and why I do this.

05The person behind it

Hi, I’m Noah.

Founder, Signet Works · Tilburg

I study Entrepreneurship here in Tilburg, and I’ve lived across North America and Northern, Western and Eastern Europe along the way.

My first client was my own business: Medixis, a company I co-founded. Building its website showed me how much a good site matters to a young business, and how much most people get charged for one.

Signet Works is my answer: premium websites at fair prices, built by the person you actually talk to. I believe in honest work, fair prices, and doing what I say I’ll do.

In or around Tilburg? I’m happy to meet in person. Let’s talk

06The philosophy behind it all

Professional websites shouldn’t be crazy expensive, or out of reach, in a digital age.

I started Signet Works on a simple idea: a professional website is no longer a luxury, so it shouldn’t be priced like one. Least of all for startups and small businesses, who often need a good website the most and can afford an expensive one the least.

AI takes care of the slow, repetitive parts of building a site, so my time goes into what matters: the design, the details, and getting your business across clearly. That’s how I can offer premium design at a price that makes sense, for businesses and for personal sites.

  • One personYou talk to me, and I build your site. No account managers, no hand-offs.
  • Fair pricesFrom about €300, with a fixed quote before any work begins.
  • Built for real businessesNot for awards. A site your customers understand, and that brings them to you.
Your website is often the first thing a customer sees. It should say, plainly and honestly, “you’re in good hands.”
  • You work with one personThe person you talk to is the person building your site.
  • Plain languageNo jargon, no upsells — just what you need and why.
  • A clear quote up frontYou know the price and the scope before work begins.
  • It’s yoursYour domain, your content, your site. No lock-in.

07Origin

Headquartered in Tilburg, operating around the globe.

Tilburg is home base. Local businesses can meet me in person, and I work with clients further away remotely.

It starts from one point on the map and keeps reaching further out.

08The name

Why a signet?

A signet ring was once used to seal a letter: a small, personal mark that said who it came from and that it could be trusted.

That’s the idea behind the name. Every site I build carries my mark, whether it’s a one-page site for a local shop or a full redesign, and I want it to earn your customers’ trust.
