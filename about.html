04About

Good websites, made honestly.

Signet Works is a small studio for businesses that need a professional website without the agency runaround.

Your website is often the first thing a customer sees. It should say, plainly and honestly, “you’re in good hands.”
  • You work with one personThe person you talk to is the person building your site.
  • Plain languageNo jargon, no upsells — just what you need and why.

  • It’s yoursYour domain, your content, your site. No lock-in.

05The name

Why a signet?

A signet ring was once used to seal a letter: a small, personal mark that said who it came from and that it could be trusted.

That is the idea behind Signet Works. Every site carries the same care, whether it is a one-page site for a local shop or a full redesign, and it is built to earn your customers’ trust.
