05Questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the size of the site and what it needs to do. After a short conversation you’ll get a fixed quote, so there are no surprises later.

How long does it take?

That depends on scope and how quickly content and feedback come together. You’ll get a realistic timeline with your quote.

I already have a website. Can you redesign it?

Yes. Redesigns keep what’s working, fix what isn’t, and move your existing content across.

Do I need to write the text and supply photos?

Bring what you have. Signet Works can help shape the wording and advise on images so the site reads well and looks consistent.

What happens after launch?

You get a walkthrough of the finished site, and ongoing updates and support are available whenever you need them.
